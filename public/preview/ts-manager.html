Extra ideas in these designs Tell me which numbers to keep

  • IDEA 1Time on jobs. Compares hours logged in with hours on job timers, so you see who is logged in but not working on jobs.
  • IDEA 2Leave clash warning and "Who's off". Staff see who else is off before they ask, and you see clashes before you approve.
  • IDEA 3Cover person. Staff name who looks after urgent jobs while they're away; that person gets their reminders.
  • IDEA 4Breaks. A Start break button for the 45 min break. Break time isn't counted as work and doesn't trigger the idle pop-up.
  • IDEA 5Forgot to log out. If someone is still logged in at 7 pm the day is closed at their last job time and flagged for you to check.
  • IDEA 6Export for payroll. One spreadsheet with each person's hours, leave by type and late starts for the month.
  • IDEA 7Extra hours. Hours over 8h 15m a day add up as extra time, shown per person each month (for time in lieu or overtime).
  • IDEA 8Public holidays. Add each office's public holidays once a year (India and Australia differ) so they don't count as leave or absence.
